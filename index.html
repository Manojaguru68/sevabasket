Questions people ask

Is delivery really free? How does the service survive?

Yes — free for buyers and free for stores. SevaBasket is a public welfare project, not a profit business. It runs on a simple, low-cost model: stores pack, local partners deliver, and the platform takes no commission, no delivery fee and no platform fee from anyone.

Are these live prices from Blinkit, Zepto and the other apps?

No app in India offers a public live price feed, so no honest website can show their real-time prices. Prices here are clearly labelled typical market prices — the normal shelf price of each item in Indian shops in November 2026. The comparison section uses only publicly available information about each app (delivery promise, typical fees, coverage model).

My town is small. Will delivery work there?

That is exactly who this is built for. Big apps serve big cities first; SevaBasket's directory covers all 36 states and UTs and 777 districts, and any local store or delivery partner in any town can join free — so service grows town by town, including places the big apps never reach.

How do I pay?

Directly to the store — cash on delivery or UPI to the store's own account. SevaBasket never collects or holds your money.

What if an item is missing or damaged?

Tell the delivery partner at the door or contact the store from your order summary — you simply don't pay for anything you didn't receive in good condition.
